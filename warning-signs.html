Warning Signs

One warning sign is enough reason to slow down and verify the offer independently.

Pressure to pay quickly

Be cautious if someone demands urgent payment or asks you to send money before verifying a job.

Vague job detials

Always ask about the employer, job description, pay, location, and a written contract.

Suspicious agency name

Small spelling diferences matter. Always check the name of the agency against the official records.

No physical location

This greatly weakens the agencie's credibility.

If any of the above applies to your agency, check the following pages for further assistance. Report Scam or Help & Contacts.
